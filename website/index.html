Where manual work still leaks through

  • SaaS stack without integration

    CRM, billing, and scheduling run in separate tools. Data gets re-keyed between systems after every customer event.

  • Legacy ERP + cloud analytics

    On-prem transactional data never reaches the warehouse on time. Reports lag operational reality by days.

  • Alert fatigue from brittle scripts

    Homegrown polling jobs break on schema changes. Notifications either flood channels or stop firing entirely.

FAQ

Common questions

What stack do you work with?

Microsoft Fabric and Power BI are the core — SQL, DAX, Power Query, and Python for pipelines, modeling, and ML. I also work with Snowflake, BigQuery, Tableau, dbt-style transformations, and Power Automate for automations. The stack follows your environment, not the other way around.

Do you work solo or with our team?

Data Foundry is one senior specialist — you get the same person from discovery through delivery. I work embedded with your analysts, IT, and business teams: leading when needed, pairing when it helps, and handing everything over documented and maintainable.

What languages do you work in?

French, English, and Spanish — native or full professional proficiency. Engagements, documentation, and training can run in any of the three, which suits Quebec, Canadian, and international teams.

What types of engagements do you offer?

Three formats, depending on where you are: a fixed-scope project (build or migration), a time-boxed assessment (audit your stack and roadmap), or a retainer for ongoing architecture, governance, and delivery support. Book a call to scope it — no cookie-cutter packages.

How do you handle messy source data?

Every flow includes validation steps, default-value logic, and explicit error branches. Missing fields, duplicate webhook payloads, and API rate limits are handled in the pipeline — not left for someone to catch manually.

What automation tools do you work with?

Zapier for SaaS-to-SaaS connections, Microsoft Power Automate for Microsoft 365 and Azure environments, and custom webhook endpoints for systems without native connectors. Platform choice follows your stack — not the other way around.
