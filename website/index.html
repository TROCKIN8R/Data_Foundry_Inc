Our Services

Bronze

Data Ingestion & Pipelines

Your sources flow reliably — governed, cataloged, and trustworthy before anything downstream touches them.

  • APIs, databases, files, and SaaS into a clean bronze layer
  • Ingest policies, scheduling, and failure alerting
  • Catalog entries from the first load — no retrofitting
→

Silver

Transformation & Modeling

Raw data shaped into dimensional models your whole org builds on — one version of truth, no more conflicting numbers.

  • Cleaned, standardized silver layer with quality checks
  • Dimensional models and semantic layer in Fabric or dbt
  • Auditable logic — every transformation documented
→

Gold

Reporting & Analytics

Boardroom-ready dashboards executives trust — built on a certified SSoT so every number means the same thing.

  • Power BI dashboards and KPIs tied to your single source
  • Report templates so new builds stay consistent and on-brand
  • Self-documenting DAX measures — auditable and maintainable
→

Gold

Self-service BI

Business users pull answers themselves — from governed data, in the tools they already use, without waiting on IT.

  • Semantic models connecting directly to Excel and Power BI
  • No shadow copies, broken pivots, or stale exports
  • Adoption support so the change actually sticks
→

Every layer

Governance & Quality

Data people actually trust — documented, owned, and enforced as a daily practice, not a one-time audit.

  • Lineage, catalog, and ownership across every layer
  • Automated quality checks with threshold alerting
  • Access control and compliance baked in from the start
→

Platinum

Agents & Automations

Workflows and AI agents that run on data you trust — and keep running long after the engagement ends.

  • Automations in Power Automate, Fabric, or custom pipelines
  • AI agents grounded in your gold-layer SSoT
  • Documented, maintainable — and fully handed over to you
